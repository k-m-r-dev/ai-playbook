# Natural Scope reference

Source: StoryScope (Russell, Rajendhran, Pham, Iyyer, Wieting), COLM 2026, arXiv:2604.03136v6. Parallel corpus of 10,272 prompts, each written by a human and by Claude Sonnet 4.6, GPT-5.4, Gemini 3 Flash, DeepSeek V3.2, and Kimi K2.5 (61,608 stories). Narrative features alone scored 93.2% macro-F1 for human vs AI. The five models occupy one cluster. Human mean rarity percentile 0.71 vs 0.49 for AI.

Gaps are human minus the five-model average. A negative gap means AI is higher. The rules in SKILL.md are binding. This file is the measurement behind them.

## Model fingerprints that must not be the spine

- Claude: event intensity escalates less than any other source. Reverent stance in 62% of stories vs 39–56% elsewhere. Epilogues, no dreams, quiet endings.
- GPT: gossip or rumor as plot in 64% vs 44–55%. Looking back from years later. Subverts expectations 41% vs 27–36%.
- Gemini: bleak, oppressive settings in 88% of stories, then tidy extended denouements.
- DeepSeek: front-loads context other sources hold back. Visible narrator explains up front.
- Kimi: opens in the middle of an action and otherwise sits at the center of the AI cluster.
