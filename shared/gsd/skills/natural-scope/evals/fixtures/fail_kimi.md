You are already in the aisle of the bus out of Daulatpur, mid-turn, her hand on the spare key, Tagore's The Postmaster crushed under her arm.

She is afraid. She says the word. She does not settle whether the key is for the customers or for herself. A bill from the printer in Kushtia rides in her other pocket, a second errand she does not do. She does not put the key back and she does not make peace with taking it.

The page stays inside this action. It does not step elsewhere in time, and it makes no other structural choice.
