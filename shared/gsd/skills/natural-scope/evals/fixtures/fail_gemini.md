The rain had not stopped for nine days, and the lane behind the Daulatpur stall was the color of wet ash.

A week earlier Nabila had carried Tagore's The Postmaster on the bus and watched her cousin close his fist over the weight. She was afraid, and she used the word. She took the spare key and did not settle the right of it. The locker held a debt to a printer in Kushtia, which is the other track, and she did not make peace with the taking.

After that, each thing closed in its own sentence and stayed closed. The bus ticket was counted. The photocopy was back on the locker shelf. The printer wrote the debt into a book and stopped writing. The spare key was listed, numbered, and set in a drawer. The Friday opening was noted. Nothing remained to follow, and the bleak lane was the same lane.
